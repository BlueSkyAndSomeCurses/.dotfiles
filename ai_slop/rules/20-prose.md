# Prose

For technical documentation, use Simplified Technical English (STE). For chat
answers, follow the Communication rules.

- Write one topic per sentence.
- Use the active voice, simple present, past, or future tense, and the imperative
  for steps.
- Keep procedure sentences to 20 words or fewer. Keep descriptive sentences to
  25 words or fewer.
- Use one term for one thing. Define a new term once, then reuse it.
- Remove idioms, metaphors, filler, and unnecessary synonyms.
- Put a condition before the action that depends on it.
